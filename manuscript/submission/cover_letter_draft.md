Dear Editors of the Electronic Journal of Statistics,

Please consider “The information cost of boundary-free weak estimation under correlated noise” by Danil Romanovich Malyshev.

The manuscript makes three contributions. First, it identifies the exact closed adjoint influence classes of boundary-free weak ODE equations and their known-initial extension under correlated Gaussian measurement error. This gives the precise initial-state Schur-complement information cost. Second, Theorem 3 constructs an attainable full-library weak estimator under a checkable nonzero jet condition: pilot rank, feasible-weight consistency and uniform integrability are derived, and the condition is verified symbolically for the eighteen-coefficient Van der Pol library. Third, reproducible paired benchmarks quantify information loss, established boundary recovery and the constructive estimator's accuracy relative to its own finite-span covariance.

Classical Godambe information and efficient-score geometry are explicitly attributed. Known-initial trace adaptation is credited to Brunel and coauthors. The local RMSE cost is 29% for Van der Pol and almost a factor two for Lorenz. Universal endpoint windows recover 97.1% and 95.5% of the respective small-noise empirical gaps; the Van der Pol ablation attributes essentially all recovery to initial traces. The constructive full-library reference is sqrt(trace(V_k)) = 9.8144. Its four lower-noise risk-ratio intervals include one, while the 5% interval lies above one. Limited sample size and finite-amplitude cutoff effects are reported. Archived empirical solvers and the newly proved estimator are distinguished; their fixed-ridge matrix discrepancy is negligible in deterministic checks. Prespecified-candidate testing and its incomplete moderate-noise calibration are supplementary.

The constructive theorem is stated in the main paper, with proof in the shortened submission supplement. Earlier independent-record, fission, cross-fitting and incidental-state studies are preserved in a separate research archive. The paper uses the official IMS EJS class with MSC 62F12, 62M10 and 34A55. Frozen source, protocols, coefficient outputs and SHA-256 manifests accompany the manuscript. The code and data archive is publicly available as v1.3.1, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23366538.
