Dear Editors of the Electronic Journal of Statistics,

Please consider “The information cost of boundary-free weak estimation under correlated noise” by Danil Romanovich Malyshev.

The manuscript makes three contributions. First, it identifies the exact closed adjoint influence classes of boundary-free weak ODE equations and their known-initial extension under correlated Gaussian measurement error. This gives the precise initial-state Schur-complement information cost. Second, Theorem 3 constructs an attainable full-library weak estimator under a checkable nonzero jet condition: pilot rank, feasible-weight consistency and uniform integrability are derived, and the condition is verified by exact arithmetic for the 18-coefficient Van der Pol library. Third, reproducible paired benchmarks quantify information loss, established boundary recovery and prespecified coefficient testing, distinguishing their statistical tasks and finite-noise limitations.

Classical Godambe information and efficient-score geometry are explicitly attributed. Known-initial trace adaptation is credited to Brunel and coauthors. The local RMSE cost is 29% for Van der Pol and almost a factor two for Lorenz. Universal endpoint windows recover 97.1% and 95.5% of the respective small-noise empirical gaps; the Van der Pol ablation attributes essentially all recovery to the established initial-trace mechanism. Recovery at 5% noise is incomplete. Independently frozen small-noise Wald validation gives 105/2000 null rejections, with exact 95% interval 4.31–6.32%, inside the stated tolerance. Power passes its tolerance criterion at one reference unit and fails it at half a unit. This is not presented as exact power equality or general superiority over sparse structure selection.

The constructive theorem is stated in the main paper, with proof in the shortened submission supplement. Earlier independent-record, fission, cross-fitting and incidental-state studies are preserved in a separate research archive. The paper uses the official IMS EJS class with MSC 62F12, 62M10 and 34A55. Frozen source, protocols, coefficient outputs and SHA-256 manifests accompany the manuscript.
