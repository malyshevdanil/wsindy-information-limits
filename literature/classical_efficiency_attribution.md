# Classical foundations of the weak-class result

- **Godambe (1960)** supplies the optimal-estimating-function framework.
  The local weak GLS information `D' Sigma^-1 D` is named accordingly.
  Original bibliographic record: [IMS publication](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177705693).
  The publisher's full text was unavailable during this audit; its metadata
  and historical role were checked against the
  [University of Waterloo author record](https://uwaterloo.ca/statistics-and-actuarial-science/contacts/vp-godambe).
- **Bickel, Klaassen, Ritov and Wellner (1993)** is cited for efficient-score
  projection and nuisance tangent spaces. The original edition and later
  reprint are distinguished using
  [Wellner's book record](https://faculty.washington.edu/jonw/RESEARCH/BOOKS/book3.html).
- **van der Vaart (1998)** is cited for Z-estimator linearization and
  nuisance-score projection, Chapters 5 and 25. Publication metadata and
  chapter contents were checked in the
  [Cambridge front matter](https://assets.cambridge.org/97805217/84504/frontmatter/9780521784504_frontmatter.pdf).
- **Dette, Konstantinou and Zhigljavsky (2017)** derives continuous-time BLUE
  and discrete approximations for correlated regression. Theorem 2.1 and
  Remark 2.1 give the continuous estimator and its endpoint representation;
  Section 3 treats discretization. Checked against the
  [author manuscript](https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/id/eprint/100907/7/detZ.pdf).

These establish the statistical tools used by the article. The specific
result retained as the contribution is the adjoint range and covariance-norm
closure of the dynamical weak class, together with its quantitative OU
benchmarks. Initial-trace adaptation remains credited to Brunel et al.
No general sandwich formula, efficient-score projection, continuous-time
BLUE or boundary remedy is claimed as newly invented here.
